# Job before moonshot

Brin led Google X, which ran the self-driving car project that became Waymo and the Loon balloons that shut down in 2021. The owner's not-to-copy is Glass: on sale in 2013 at $1,500 before its use was proven, withdrawn from consumers in January 2015. Never put a product in front of members before its job is clear.

## When to Activate

- A big new product is proposed: in-game model, AI assistant, app, device or new line.
- The owner says "moonshot", "ten times better" or "the future of the company".
- Before anything new is priced, sold or shown to members.

Do not use for:
- capping the loss of an experiment: tailered-ai:capped-experiment;
- choosing between efforts when cash is short: tailered-ai:runway-triage;
- a feature from a member's ask, nothing built yet: tailered-ai:press-release-first;
- running the opt-in member test: tailered-ai:labs-opt-in.

## Inputs and outputs

Where to work: create a task folder, `artifacts/<skill name>-<YYYYMMDD>/` in the current project (this session's scratchpad if the project is read-only), and write the output there. Tables go in your reply.

Inputs: the idea, who it is for, and any member asks or usage behind it.

Missing inputs never leave a blank. Put in a concrete proposal from what you know, mark it `proposal`, finish every step, and end with the one or two inputs that would change the result. A proposal is a real value: a dollar figure, a name, a date, a game, a list of likely items. Writing missing, pending, unknown, TBD or $X in its place is still a blank.

Output: `job-gate.md`: the job sentence, the evidence, the first milestone, and the gate verdict.

## Steps

Lead the reply with the job sentence and the evidence table, then the verdict line, then the detail.

### 1. Write the job

One sentence: who (such as a partner market trader on NBA totals), what they try to do, what they use today, and what goes wrong. If it cannot be written, the job is not clear.

Completion: one sentence with a named member type and today's workaround.

### 2. Show the job is real

Give evidence a skeptic would accept: member asks with their source, use of the nearest feature, or five member talks. Give a number, such as 12 asks in 30 days.

Completion: at least one number with its source, or marked `proposal` with how to get it.

### 3. Set the first milestone

Name a milestone that needs no member to see anything: a backtest, an internal prototype, a staff-only test. Give its date and cost; cap the cost with tailered-ai:capped-experiment.

Completion: milestone, date and cost.

### 4. Gate it

Hold when the job is not clear. Prove when the job is clear and the milestone is next. Stage when the milestone passed: it may go to members only through tailered-ai:labs-opt-in, labeled, opt-in and capped. No price, sale or general launch before then. The owner decides; name the owner.

Completion: hold, prove or stage, with the owner.

## Anti-Patterns

- A price on a product whose job nobody can write.
- Demos to members before the milestone passed.
- "Members will figure out what it is for."
- Treating a hold as failure.
