# Teach every launch

Every launch teaches how the thing works. Huang's keynotes explain the technology at length, why it works and what it changes, before anything is sold. The guard is the owner's not-to-copy: in 2022 the SEC settled charges that NVIDIA had not disclosed that crypto mining drove much of its gaming growth, and NVIDIA paid a $5.5 million penalty. Teaching in public here means every public number carries its source, and what drove a result is said even when it flatters less.

## When to Activate

- A model version, league or main product feature is about to launch, or a season starts.
- The owner wants a public launch post, talk, video script or explainer of how the model works.
- A public post would show a number, a record or an edge.

Do not use for:
- explaining a number to the team inside the company: tailered-ai:know-the-numbers;
- designing a public test with frozen entries: tailered-ai:prove-it-in-public;
- a single image: tailered-ai:graphic;
- the release calendar and slip notes: tailered-ai:yearly-model-clock.

## Inputs and outputs

Where to work: create a task folder, `artifacts/<skill name>-<YYYYMMDD>/` in the current project (this session's scratchpad if the project is read-only), and write the output there. Tables go in your reply.

Inputs: what is launching, the audience (mostly prediction-market traders, some sportsbook bettors), and the numbers available with their records.

Missing inputs never leave a blank. Put in a concrete proposal from what you know, mark it `proposal`, finish every step, and end with the one or two inputs that would change the result. A proposal is a real value: a dollar figure, a name, a date, a game, a list of likely items. Writing missing, pending, unknown, TBD or $X in its place is still a blank.

Output: `explainer.md`: the one lesson, the draft, a source table for every number, and the verdict.

## Steps

### 1. Pick the one lesson

Write the one idea a member should understand afterward, such as how a projected total is built or why the model's price differs from a partner source's.

Completion: the lesson in one sentence.

### 2. Draft the explainer

Use plain words and one worked example from a real game. Keep it to 600 words or five minutes unless the owner sets another length. Call the output estimates, never advice.

Completion: a draft in `explainer.md` within the length.

### 3. Source every number

Give each number its source: file or query, date range, sample size. Say what drove the result, such as one league, a short stretch or one market type. Cut any number that cannot be sourced.

Completion: a source table with every number in the draft, and zero numbers without a source.

### 4. Decide and route

Verdict: publish, change or stop. Publishing is public content: the owner approves the words, and it goes live only through tailered-ai:workflow and the release coordinator on the owner's word. Nothing is posted from here.

Completion: the verdict word, the owner named as approver, and the release route.

## Anti-Patterns

- A record with no date range or sample size.
- Leaving out the one league or stretch that drove the result.
- Selling before teaching.
- Calling projections picks.
