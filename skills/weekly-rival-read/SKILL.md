# Weekly rival read

Go look at the rival yourself. Walton said he spent more time in rivals' stores than in his own, and he said openly that most of his ideas were borrowed. At the company one person reads the rival sites each week and brings back one copy-or-skip item, with credit. The guard: Hypermart USA, a copy of the European hypermarket, was reported as too big for its shoppers. A copied idea must fit our members, about 70% prediction-market traders and 30% sportsbook bettors.

## When to Activate

- Nobody has read rival sites in the last week.
- The owner says "go look at the rival", "what are they doing" or "rival read".
- A rival launch, price change or new market is in the news.

Do not use for:
- building our own version of a chosen feature: tailered-ai:copy-then-outgrow;
- a feature that starts from a member's ask: tailered-ai:press-release-first;
- a public accuracy contest against a rival: tailered-ai:prove-it-in-public.

## Inputs and outputs

Where to work: create a task folder, `artifacts/<skill name>-<YYYYMMDD>/` in the current project (this session's scratchpad if the project is read-only), and write the output there. Tables go in your reply.

Inputs: this week's reader, the rival list, and last week's read if one exists.

Missing inputs never leave a blank. Put in a concrete proposal from what you know, mark it `proposal`, finish every step, and end with the one or two inputs that would change the result. A proposal is a real value: a dollar figure, a name, a date, a game, a list of likely items. Writing missing, pending, unknown, TBD or $X in its place is still a blank.

Output: `rival-read.md`: the reader, the sites read, what changed on each, the one item and its verdict.

## Steps

Lead the reply with the one item and its copy or skip verdict, then the site table.

### 1. One reader, one week

Name the one person or session reading this week. List three to six rivals (proposal: Action Network, OddsJam, Unabated, and the Partner A and Partner B sports pages).

Completion: reader, week dates and the site list with addresses are written.

### 2. Read as a visitor

Read public pages only. Create no account, start no trial, sign in to nothing. Note what changed since last week's read.

Completion: one dated line per site saying what was seen and on which page.

### 3. Bring one item

Pick the single best idea and write it plainly with credit: "Rival does this." Judge it for our members: would a prediction-market trader use it here as they do there? Does it fit a projections product that never gives picks?

Completion: exactly one item, the verdict copy or skip, a reason in member terms, and the rival credited by name.

### 4. Hand it on

A copy goes to tailered-ai:copy-then-outgrow to plan the build, with an owner. A skip is logged with a date before which it is not raised again (proposal: eight weeks). Nothing is built or shipped here; any live change later goes through tailered-ai:workflow and the release coordinator on the owner's word.

Completion: a named owner for the copy, or the skip logged with its date.

## Anti-Patterns

- Bringing a list of ten ideas instead of one.
- Copying without saying where the idea came from.
- Signing up or paying to see a rival's product.
- Copying a feature our members would not use.
