# Split the bets

In 2015 Page split Google into Alphabet: bets such as Waymo and Verily got their own chiefs, and Alphabet reports Other Bets apart from Google. The guard: Other Bets reported operating losses in the billions of dollars each year. Splitting shows the cost; it does not cut it. Each split needs a budget, a review date and a kill number.

## When to Activate

- A side project or venture grows inside the core main product budget, and nobody knows its cost.
- The owner says spin it out, Alphabet or other bets.
- A bet has run a year with no review.

Do not use for:
- naming one owner for one initiative: tailered-ai:single-threaded-owner;
- capping one trial: tailered-ai:capped-experiment.

## Inputs and outputs

Where to work: create a task folder, `artifacts/<skill name>-<YYYYMMDD>/` in the current project (this session's scratchpad if the project is read-only), and write the output there. Tables go in your reply.

Inputs: the work running, its monthly cost in money and hours, its results and its leader.

Missing inputs never leave a blank. Put in a concrete proposal from what you know, mark it `proposal`, finish every step, and end with the one or two inputs that would change the result. A proposal is a real value: a dollar figure, a name, a date, a game, a list of likely items. Writing missing, pending, unknown, TBD or $X in its place is still a blank.

Output: `bets.md`: the core and each bet with leader, budget, borrow limit, review, kill number and verdict.

## Steps

Lead the reply with the core-and-bets table, then the verdict line, detail after.

### 1. Name the core and list the bets

The core: projections for NBA, NHL, college football and college basketball on the main product, the day build and the publishing step. Everything else is a bet, such as a creator program, a bankroll tool or a new sport. For each write monthly cost in dollars and hours and a result number.

Completion: a table of the core and every bet with cost and result number.

### 2. Draw the line

For each bet name its leader, where its costs are logged, its monthly budget and the hours it may borrow from the core, such as day-build time.

Completion: each bet has a leader, a ledger, a budget and a written borrow limit.

### 3. Set the review

Give each bet a review date (proposal: every quarter) and a kill number, such as under 50 active users by the review. Report bet losses in the same table as the core.

Completion: a review date and kill number per bet, and losses shown beside the core.

### 4. Verdict and route

Write keep, change (fold back into the core or narrow it) or stop per bet. The owner sets budgets and decides. Nothing is moved, spent or shut down here; any live change ships through tailered-ai:workflow and the release coordinator on the owner's word.

Completion: a verdict word per bet, the owner named as the decider, and the release route.

## Anti-Patterns

- A split with no budget or kill number.
- Bets borrowing core people or machines with no written limit.
- Reporting the core and hiding bet losses.
- So many bets that no leader has time for one.
