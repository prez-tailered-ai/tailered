# Buy-early watchlist

Google bought Android in 2005 for a reported $50 million, YouTube in 2006 for $1.65 billion in stock, and DeepMind in 2014 for a reported $500 million, each before its price showed what it would become. The company adopts early instead: a market, data source, method or small partner while terms are cheap. The guard: Motorola Mobility, bought in 2012 for $12.5 billion and sold in 2014 for about $2.9 billion, was big, late and outside the core. Nothing adopted shares member data without asking (Buzz).

## When to Activate

- A new market, data source, feed, method or tool is rising.
- The owner says buy early, or a cheap early deal is offered.

Do not use for:
- a tool that already beats us: tailered-ai:license-not-rebuild;
- running the trial once chosen: tailered-ai:capped-experiment.

## Inputs and outputs

Where to work: create a task folder, `artifacts/<skill name>-<YYYYMMDD>/` in the current project (this session's scratchpad if the project is read-only), and write the output there. Tables go in your reply.

Inputs: the candidates, their growth signals, the cost to adopt now and the owner's runway.

Missing inputs never leave a blank. Put in a concrete proposal from what you know, mark it `proposal`, finish every step, and end with the one or two inputs that would change the result. A proposal is a real value: a dollar figure, a name, a date, a game, a list of likely items. Writing missing, pending, unknown, TBD or $X in its place is still a blank.

Output: `watchlist.md`: up to five candidates with signal, cost, fit, trigger, verdict and review date.

## Steps

Lead the reply with the watchlist table, then the verdict line, detail after.

### 1. List the candidates

Up to five. For each write a growth signal with a number (volume, members, games covered), the cost now and a proposed cost in a year.

Completion: each candidate has a signal number and a cost now and later.

### 2. Test the fit

Does it serve the main product's job, model numbers beside sportsbook and prediction-market prices, for an audience of about 70 percent prediction-market traders? Outside that it is a Motorola. Would it hold or send member data, and how would members be asked?

Completion: fit yes or no with the reason, and the member-data answer.

### 3. Set the trigger

Name the signal level where waiting costs more than acting, such as monthly volume past $50 million (proposal), and the cheapest early step: capture-only prices or a sandbox key the owner requests.

Completion: a trigger number and an early step per candidate.

### 4. Verdict and route

Write act now (a proposal to the owner), watch (with trigger and review date) or stop. Name the watchlist owner; proposal: the markets lead. Nothing is signed, bought or connected here. Spend is the owner's word, sized with tailered-ai:capped-experiment; terms go to tailered-ai:growth-cap-check; live use ships through tailered-ai:workflow and the release coordinator on the owner's word.

Completion: a verdict word per candidate, an owner and a review date.

## Anti-Patterns

- Waiting for proof until the price has risen.
- Buying big and late outside the core.
- Adopting a source that shares member data without asking.
- A watchlist with no trigger numbers.
