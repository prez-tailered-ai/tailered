# Mission is the boss

Let the mission set the order of work. Huang is reported to run NVIDIA without fixed long-range plans or a yearly planning cycle: priorities are reset as the world changes, and the mission, not a manager or a plan, decides. The guard: with no written plan, priorities reportedly follow the founder, and at the company they would follow whoever spoke last. So the mission is written, every task is tested against it, and each kept task has one owner and a date to look again.

## When to Activate

- Work for the week is being chosen with no plan in place.
- Two tasks compete and nobody can say which matters more.
- The owner says "the mission is the boss", asks what to stop, or asks whether a project still serves the company.
- Work is following whoever asked last.

Do not use for:
- a choice that trades this season against years out: tailered-ai:long-term-letter;
- checking whether metrics are proxies: tailered-ai:day-one-check;
- deciding who owns one initiative: tailered-ai:single-threaded-owner.

## Inputs and outputs

Where to work: create a task folder, `artifacts/<skill name>-<YYYYMMDD>/` in the current project (this session's scratchpad if the project is read-only), and write the output there. Tables go in your reply.

Inputs: the mission in the owner's words, and the open work (queue records, plans, session tasks) with owners.

Missing inputs never leave a blank. Put in a concrete proposal from what you know, mark it `proposal`, finish every step, and end with the one or two inputs that would change the result. A proposal is a real value: a dollar figure, a name, a date, a game, a list of likely items. Writing missing, pending, unknown, TBD or $X in its place is still a blank.

Output: `mission-sort.md`: the mission, every task with its verdict and reason, and the top three for the week.

## Steps

Lead the reply with the result: a table of every task with its owner, its verdict (keep, change or stop) and a one-line reason; then the top three, each with an owner and a look-again date. Read other sessions' queue records for this; write only your own record.

### 1. Write the mission

Use the owner's words. If none are written, propose one sentence and mark it, such as: publish the most trusted sports projections beside every price members can trade.

Completion: one sentence, marked the owner's or proposal.

### 2. List the work

List every open task from the queue records, plans and session tasks, with its owner. Read other sessions' records; never write them.

Completion: a list of tasks with an owner or "no owner" on each.

### 3. Test each task

Ask one question: does this make the projections more accurate, more trusted, or easier for members to use? Give each task keep, change or stop, with a one-line reason.

Completion: a verdict word and a reason on every task.

### 4. Set the order

Pick the top three for the week, each with an owner and a date to look again (seven days unless the owner sets another). Stops are proposals until the owner confirms them. Live work still ships through tailered-ai:workflow and the release coordinator on the owner's word.

Completion: three tasks with owners and dates, and the stops listed for the owner.

## Anti-Patterns

- Ranking by who asked loudest or last.
- A mission so broad that every task passes.
- Keeping a task with no owner.
- Stopping another session's work without the owner.
