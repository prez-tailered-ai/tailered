# Win on numbers

Windows and Office became the standard: the 1999 findings of fact put Windows on more than 90% of Intel-compatible PCs. The owner's not-to-copy: in 2000 a court ruled against using that size to squeeze rivals. The company becomes the standard only with better numbers members can check, such as accuracy against the close, calibration and freshness beside sportsbook, Partner A and Partner B prices.

## When to Activate

- The owner wants the company to be the first number checked, or to beat a rival.
- The owner says "win the market", "crush them", "lock in members" or "make us the default".
- A plan to beat rivals uses exclusivity, bundling or prices below cost.

Do not use for:
- copying a rival's feature: tailered-ai:copy-then-outgrow;
- one public accuracy test: tailered-ai:prove-it-in-public;
- setting member prices: tailered-ai:price-on-value.

## Inputs and outputs

Where to work: create a task folder, `artifacts/<skill name>-<YYYYMMDD>/` in the current project (this session's scratchpad if the project is read-only), and write the output there. Tables go in your reply.

Inputs: the rival or market, the league, and any plan.

Missing inputs never leave a blank. Put in a concrete proposal from what you know, mark it `proposal`, finish every step, and end with the one or two inputs that would change the result. A proposal is a real value: a dollar figure, a name, a date, a game, a list of likely items. Writing missing, pending, unknown, TBD or $X in its place is still a blank.

Output: `numbers-plan.md`: scoreboard, gap, struck tactics, plan, verdict.

## Steps

Your reply leads with the scoreboard (metric, the company, rival, source, date), then the verdict line: win, change or stop.

### 1. Build the scoreboard

Pick numbers members can check: closing line value, calibration, error on totals, freshness against market prices. The company's come from graded results, the rival's from public records. A missing rival number is a marked proposal with its basis.

Completion: at least three metrics, each with the company, rival, source and date.

### 2. Find the gap to close

Name the one metric that would win the most members, with about 70% prediction-market traders and 30% sportsbook bettors in mind. Set a target, such as beating the close on 52% of NBA totals over 300 games (proposal).

Completion: one metric, a target and a game count.

### 3. Strike the banned tactics

Strike anything that wins by size or terms: exclusive deals that bar a book, market or creator from rivals; tying products together; prices below cost to drive a rival out; making export or cancelling hard.

Completion: the struck list, or "none found".

### 4. Plan, owner, route

Write the plan with a named owner and a check date. Model ideas go to tailered-ai:ai-model-research first. A public claim waits for the graded number and goes through tailered-ai:prove-it-in-public. Live changes go through tailered-ai:workflow and the release coordinator on the owner's word. Verdict: win, change or stop.

Completion: the plan, owner, check date, route and verdict.

## Anti-Patterns

- "Lock in" meaning hard to leave.
- Claiming the standard before the scoreboard shows it.
- Choosing a metric because the company already wins it.
- Price cuts meant to starve a rival.
