# Story, not ads

Pull demand with true stories and attention, not ad spend. For years Tesla spent little on paid ads; launches and Musk's own posts pulled the demand. The owner's not-to-copy: in August 2018 Musk posted that funding was secured to take Tesla private. It was not, and the SEC settlement cost him and Tesla 20 million dollars each. A story pulls demand only when every number in it is real.

## When to Activate

- Planning how to grow members or attention, a launch, a new league or a model milestone.
- The owner asks about ads, a marketing push, going viral or what to post.
- Any public post or member note that includes a number.

Do not use for:
- designing a public test of the model: tailered-ai:prove-it-in-public;
- making the post's image: tailered-ai:graphic;
- mapping how growth compounds: tailered-ai:flywheel-map.

## Inputs and outputs

Where to work: create a task folder, `artifacts/<skill name>-<YYYYMMDD>/` in the current project (this session's scratchpad if the project is read-only), and write the output there. Tables go in your reply.

Inputs: the goal (members, signups or a launch), what really happened (results, a new league, a product moment), and any budget.

Missing inputs never leave a blank. Put in a concrete proposal from what you know, mark it `proposal`, finish every step, and end with the one or two inputs that would change the result.

Output: `story.md`: the moment, the number check table, the channel plan with its ad-spend line, the drafts and the verdict.

## Steps

### 1. Find the true moment

Pick one thing that happened that a prediction-market trader or sportsbook bettor would care about: a new league live, the model's record graded in public, a main product change members asked for. The company publishes estimates, never advice. Name the owner.

Completion: one dated moment with a named owner.

### 2. Check every number

List each number the story would use. For each, write the live source, today's value, and real or not real. A plan, a forecast, a deal not signed or a result not graded is not real, and it comes out. This is the owner's rule: never post a number before it is real.

Completion: a table in which every number is real with its source, or removed.

### 3. Plan the channels

Free channels first: the owner's own posts, member email, creator shout-outs, and prediction-market and betting communities. Any paid spend gets a dollar cap and a written reason the free channels cannot reach that audience. No promise of winnings anywhere.

Completion: a channel list with owners, and an ad-spend line of $0 or a capped amount with its reason.

### 4. Draft and hand to the owner

Write the drafts only. The owner approves and posts them. A main product or site change goes through tailered-ai:workflow and the release coordinator on the owner's word. Give a verdict: post, change or hold.

Completion: drafts saved, and post, change or hold left with the owner.

## Anti-Patterns

- Posting a number that is planned, not real.
- Buying ads before a free channel was tried.
- Promising winnings or picks.
- Posting on the owner's behalf.
