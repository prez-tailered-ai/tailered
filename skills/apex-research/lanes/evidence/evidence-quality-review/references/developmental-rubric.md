# Template rubric

A starting point to adapt, not a validated instrument. Content validity: `not_established`. Inter-rater reliability: `not_established`. Change either only with documented evidence for the exact discipline, work type, stage and use.

The upstream skill cited an experimental, literature-grounded framework for evaluating research ideas (arXiv:2510.16234). Its reported results do not transfer to this rubric, to people, or to decisions.

## Criteria and default weights

| id | Criterion | Look for | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | Question and scope | a bounded question; significance argued for the field and stage; assumptions and success conditions; feasible scope. Fashion, affiliation or venue is not significance. | 0.15 |
| C2 | Literature grounding and contribution | stated search or selection bounds; engagement with contrary work; traceable primary sources for comparisons; the dimensions a contribution is claimed on; limits on novelty claims | 0.20 |
| C3 | Method and design fit | question, design, data and method aligned; sampling and measurement choices; alternatives considered; validity threats and mitigation; ethics and governance; enough detail to check | 0.25 |
| C4 | Analysis, claims and uncertainty | methods fit the inferential target; assumptions and diagnostics; robustness and alternative explanations; uncertainty reported; claims match results; limits on generalisation and causal language | 0.20 |
| C5 | Transparency, integrity and reproducibility | complete reporting with stable locators; protocols, data, code and materials or justified restrictions; null and contrary findings; conflicts, corrections, attribution | 0.20 |

Weights are value judgments: declare and justify them before rating. Open practice is not absolute where privacy, consent, safety or legitimate restrictions apply; judge whether a restriction is justified and a safe alternative offered.

## Scale (ordinal, 0 to 4)

0 observed lack of support; 1 limited; 2 mixed; 3 substantial; 4 strong. Write criterion-specific anchor text for each point and rate against the text, not the number. Never map a score to accept or reject, excellence labels, predicted impact or any decision about a person.

## Statuses

- `rated`: score, uncertainty range (bounded, rater-supplied), evidence ids, rationale reference.
- `missing`: needed evidence absent or unavailable; score and range null.
- `not_applicable`: documented reason; score and range null.

## Composite, only when justified

Descriptive score = sum of weight times score over rated criteria, divided by the sum of their weights. Report beside it: the weight in each status (all, applicable, rated, missing, not applicable), what share of applicable weight was rated, the contribution of every criterion, and the aggregated uncertainty range (not a confidence interval). Normalising does not make incomplete evaluations comparable.

Sensitivity: raise and lower one weight at a time, renormalise, and report the score range and whether any ordering between works changes. Instability means the order depends on contestable weights. Stability does not show validity.

## Rater protocol

Qualified raters; conflicts disclosed and recusals made; training on construct, anchors, evidence rules and missingness; calibration on synthetic or authorised examples; independent rating before discussion; evidence ids and ranges recorded; disagreements investigated and resolved by evidence, not averaging; drift watched across rounds.

## Evidence manifest entry

Evidence id (pseudonymous), linked criterion ids, source type, local stable locator, local claim reference, access status, verification status. No excerpts of private documents.

## Process audit checklist

Construct and rubric provenance; validity evidence and limits; rater training and agreement; traceability and source access; missingness and not-applicable reasons; weight sensitivity; bias review by discipline and subgroup; recusals for conflicts; accommodations for access needs; privacy, minimisation and retention; correction and appeal route. Each item is confirmed with a reference or fails closed.
