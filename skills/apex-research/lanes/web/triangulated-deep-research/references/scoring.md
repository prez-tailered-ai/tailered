# Source scoring and source types

Detail for step 6 of triangulated-deep-research. Scores are recorded on the `source` entry as one line each with the reason. They inform the disposition; they never replace citation-audit.

## Credibility

- high: primary record (filing, official statistics, specification, dataset), peer-reviewed study, or an author with direct access and a track record.
- medium: reputable reporting or analysis that cites its basis.
- low: unsourced assertion, anonymous claim, marketing copy, content farm.

## Recency

- current: dated inside the protocol window, and nothing later supersedes it.
- aging: older but the facts it reports are slow to change; say why.
- stale: outside the window for a fast-moving fact, or superseded.

Retrieval time is not the source date.

## Bias

- low: no stake in the conclusion, or the stake is disclosed and the method is open.
- moderate: some stake (vendor-funded study, partisan outlet) but checkable data.
- high: direct stake and no checkable data.

Note shared funders or owners across sources; they reduce independence as well as raising bias.

## Source types for diversity

| Type | Examples |
|---|---|
| primary | filings, official data, standards, source code, first-party announcements |
| academic | peer-reviewed papers, preprints (labelled), theses |
| industry | analyst reports, trade press, vendor benchmarks (labelled) |
| discussion | practitioner forums, interviews, issue trackers |

A thesis supported by one type only, even from several groups, is reported with that limit beside it.

## Genres and their blocks

- question and answer: answer, evidence, caveats.
- explainer: mechanism, actors, how it changes.
- decision: options, criteria, evidence per option, recommendation labelled as such, counter-case.
- landscape: map of entrants, segments, movements, gaps.
- validation: hypotheses, tests, results, status.
- custom: state the blocks in the plan.
