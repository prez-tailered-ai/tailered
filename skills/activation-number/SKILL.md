# Activation number

Find the one early action that predicts a new member stays, then move that number. Facebook's growth team, formed around 2007, owned sign-up, activation and retention as one funnel, with a reported early target of seven friends in ten days. The guard is the owner's not-to-copy: the 2019 FTC order fined Facebook $5 billion, and its complaint included login phone numbers used for ads. Member data collected for one purpose is never used for another, such as marketing, without consent.

## When to Activate

- New members sign up but do not come back.
- The owner asks about activation, onboarding, retention, churn or a growth team.
- A growth idea would use member emails, phone numbers, payment or usage data.

Do not use for:
- drawing the growth loop: tailered-ai:flywheel-map;
- reading a partner's or platform's terms: tailered-ai:growth-cap-check;
- a new main product feature with nothing built: tailered-ai:press-release-first.

## Inputs and outputs

Where to work: create a task folder, `artifacts/<skill name>-<YYYYMMDD>/` in the current project (this session's scratchpad if the project is read-only), and write the output there. Tables go in your reply.

Inputs: sign-ups by week, what each new member did in the first seven days, and who was still active in week four.

Missing inputs never leave a blank. Put in a concrete proposal from what you know, mark it `proposal`, finish every step, and end with the one or two inputs that would change the result.

Output: `activation.md`: the funnel table, the activation number, one fix with its owner, the data-purpose check, and the verdict.

## Steps

### 1. Draw the funnel

Count four stages for the last four or more weekly cohorts: signed up, did the first useful thing (followed a league, opened a projection beside a market price), came back in week two, active in week four. Give each stage a count and a rate.

Completion: a table with counts and rates for each cohort.

### 2. Find the activation number

Compare members who stayed with those who left. Pick the one early action, with a count and a day limit, that best separates them, such as opened projections on three days in the first week. With too few members to tell, propose one and mark it `proposal`.

Completion: one sentence with an action, a count and a day limit, plus the stay rate above and below it.

### 3. Check every data use

List each piece of member data the fix would use and the purpose it was collected for. Login phone, sign-in email and payment details serve login, receipts and billing only. Any other use, such as marketing, needs the member's recorded consent first; without it, drop that part of the fix.

Completion: a table of data, collected purpose, planned use and consent, with no row marked use without consent.

### 4. Pick one fix and judge it

Choose one change aimed at the activation number. Name its owner and the target rate by a date. At the date give keep, change or stop with the number. Anything members see ships through tailered-ai:workflow and the release coordinator on the owner's word; this skill sends no message.

Completion: the fix, owner, target, date, and the release route named.

## Anti-Patterns

- Counting sign-ups as growth.
- Using login phone numbers or emails for marketing without consent.
- Changing five things at once, so nothing can be read.
- Sending a re-engagement blast from this skill.
