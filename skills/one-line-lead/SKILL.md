# One-line lead

Jobs carried simplicity through every surface; the iPod (2001) was introduced in one line, a thousand songs in your pocket. The owner: lead every card with one checkable line, details one tap deeper. The guard: the iPhone 4's clean antenna lost signal when held a certain way (2010). Simple must still work: the number keeps its source.

## When to Activate

- Card words are written, reviewed or cut.
- The owner says "one line", "lead line", "too much on the card" or "one tap deeper".
- A card's first line is a slogan, reads like a pick, or cannot be checked.

Do not use for:
- the card's layout, styling or build: tailered-ai:design-craft;
- planning a new card: tailered-ai:design-shape.

## Inputs and outputs

Where to work: create a task folder, `artifacts/<skill name>-<YYYYMMDD>/` in the current project (this session's scratchpad if the project is read-only), and write the output there. Tables go in your reply.

Inputs: the card, its current words, and the data fields behind it.

Missing inputs never leave a blank. Put in a concrete proposal from what you know, mark it `proposal`, finish every step, and end with the one or two inputs that would change the result. A proposal is a real value: a dollar figure, a name, a date, a game, a list of likely items. Writing missing, pending, unknown, TBD or $X in its place is still a blank. When you cannot open the page, file or data a step asks for, still fill every row: name the exact page, file or command to check and the value you expect there, marked `proposal`. Every row of every table gets a verdict word and an owner.

Output: `lead-line.md`: the line, its data fields, the call on every other line, and the copy check.

## Steps

Lead the reply with the proposed line and its field table, then the verdict line, then detail.

### 1. Pick the one fact

Choose the fact a member checks first, usually the model's number beside the best market price: model total 221.5, a partner source 218.5. The teams row stays on top; the lead line comes right under it.

Completion: the fact named, with the data field for each number.

### 2. Write the line

One line, at most 60 characters (proposal), one number pair and its source. Projections only: no pick words (bet, lock, take), verdict, slogan, badge or disclaimer. A started game is never shown as pregame.

Completion: the line, its character count, and each number tied to a data field.

### 3. Move the rest one tap deeper

Mark every other line keep in the disclosure, move, or cut. Lean and timing sit inside Book vs model. No model-verdict footer or source-note paragraphs. Keep control labels and real errors.

Completion: every other line has keep, move or cut.

### 4. Check the copy

Route the copy check through tailered-ai:design-craft. Run the project's copy check and look at the rendered route with disclosures open. Name the owner. The change ships through tailered-ai:workflow and the release coordinator on the owner's word.

Completion: the check result as run (pass, fail, or not run with the reason), the owner, and the release route named.

## Anti-Patterns

- A lead line with no number.
- Pick or verdict words on a projection.
- Cutting the source or start state to look clean.
- Accepting copy on a grep or a passing build alone.
