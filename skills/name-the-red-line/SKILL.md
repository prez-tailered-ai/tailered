# Name the red line

Write the lines down before pressure comes. In January 2010 Google said it would stop censoring search in China and moved search to Hong Kong that March; Brin was reported as a leading voice. The guard: Project Dragonfly, a censored China search prototype, was reported in 2018 and dropped after protest. A line that is not written down drifts back.

## When to Activate

- Someone asks the company to publish picks, hide a rival's price or drop a losing record.
- The owner asks "should we take a stand" or "what do we stand for".
- No written list of lines exists.

Do not use for:
- keeping control of a decision: tailered-ai:keep-the-final-say;
- paid placement near the core result: tailered-ai:never-sell-the-answer;
- deciding what to post for growth: tailered-ai:story-not-ads.

## Inputs and outputs

Where to work: create a task folder, `artifacts/<skill name>-<YYYYMMDD>/` in the current project (this session's scratchpad if the project is read-only), and write the output there. Tables go in your reply.

Inputs: the request, the asker, the cost of no, and any written list of lines.

Missing inputs never leave a blank. Put in a concrete proposal from what you know, mark it `proposal`, finish every step, and end with the one or two inputs that would change the result. A proposal is a real value: a dollar figure, a name, a date, a game, a list of likely items. Writing missing, pending, unknown, TBD or $X in its place is still a blank.

Output: `red-lines.md`: the dated lines, the test, the cost of no, the verdict and the draft.

## Steps

Lead the reply with the line-by-line test table, then the verdict line, then the detail.

### 1. Write the lines

Use the written list, or propose one: estimates, never advice; the core method is never sold; every market's price is shown fairly, best first; the full record is published, losses included; member data is used only for what it was given for. Date each line.

Completion: a dated list of three to six lines, each real or marked `proposal`.

### 2. State the pressure

Who asks, what exactly, and what no costs: money per month, a partner lost, members lost. Give a number.

Completion: the asker, the request in one sentence, and the cost of no as a number.

### 3. Test it

For each line: crossed or not crossed. Refuse when any line is crossed. Accept when none is. Accept with a change when a version exists that crosses none; write that version. The owner decides.

Completion: crossed or not per line, and refuse, accept or accept-with-change.

### 4. Draft the stand

Draft a short reply to the asker and, if the owner wants it public, a member note on what the company will not do and why. Send and post nothing. Add any new line to the list with today's date.

Completion: the drafts exist, nothing was sent, and the list is updated.

## Anti-Patterns

- Deciding the line after hearing the price.
- A stand made privately, then quietly undone.
- Lines so vague any request passes.
- Posting the stand before the owner approves it.
