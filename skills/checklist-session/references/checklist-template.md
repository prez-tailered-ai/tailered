# Checklist output shape

Lead with the verified outcome and the exact boundary reached.

Legend: checked means the exact item is satisfied within its stated scope; unchecked means the requirement remains open. Fresh means executed now; Retained means saved evidence was inspected rather than rerun. Reported and Proposed are not verification results.

## 1. Context discovery and retention

- [x] **PASS — [specific completed item].** [Evidence extent and source.]
- [ ] **UNAVAILABLE / OPEN — [coverage or retention gap].** [Matching source or explicit absence of evidence.]

## 2. Changes and current configuration

- [x] **PASS — [verified change].** [Live/staged identity and source.]
- [ ] **NOT ADOPTED / CONFLICT REMAINS — [requirement].** [Current-state source.]

## 3. Executable checks and candidate integrity

- [x] **PASS / PASS WITH WARNINGS — [actual check].** [Fresh or Retained, result, command/log source and applicable warnings.]
- [ ] **FAIL / BLOCKED / INCOMPLETE — [required check].** [Failure evidence and effect on readiness.]

## 4. Review, loading and behavioral proof

- [x] **Retained — [individual review or observed path].** [Candidate identity, verdict and actual scope.]
- [ ] **OPEN — [missing behavioral requirement].** [Evidence and remaining gate.]

## 5. Adoption and completion boundary

- [x] **COMPLETE / PREPARED — [exact boundary].** [Receipt/source.]
- [ ] **OPEN / UNMEASURED — [adoption or benefit requirement].** [Evidence extent.]

Include only relevant items; the examples are not facts or mandatory work. Mark a whole irrelevant section NOT APPLICABLE with a reason rather than filling it with artificial tasks. Close with one necessary next action or a completion statement.
